# Source-to-feature mapping

| Source evidence | Product feature |
|---|---|
| NITI Aayog Strategy for New India @75 recommends marketing reforms, e-trading, direct sales, farmer-consumer markets and FPO aggregation | Marketplace, buyer access, FPO-ready workflow |
| e-NAM describes transparent bidding, more markets/buyers, real-time nearby mandi prices and online payments | Market price screen, bidding, nearest mandi |
| RBI 2019 supply-chain survey: farmer share varied 28–78% across 14 crops; mark-ups affected by charges, transport, storage, intermediaries and infrastructure | Cost-stack calculator and evidence dashboard |
| RBI 2024 TOP working paper: farmer share estimated ~33% tomato, 36% onion, 37% potato | Research cards and scenario comparison |
| NABARD 2025 FPO data: 4,585 market-linked FPOs as of 31 July 2025 | FPO / market-linkage research metric |
| MoSPI Situation Assessment 2019: average monthly agricultural-household income ₹10,218 in 2018–19 | Research context |
| Lok Sabha/MoAFW 2025: government cites TOP farmer shares and identifies marketing channels, marketing costs/margins and perishability as factors | Research caveat and policy framing |
